The banner and spotlight templates are adapted from [Hugo Story by CaressOfSteel](https://github.com/caressofsteel/hugo-story/tree/dcc16e51cc6ebd1b399e7770e090c25a20d3b8c9), a port of [Story by AJ at HTML5 UP](https://html5up.net/story). They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The project adapts the two templates for a river map, accessible figures and a custom stylesheet. The remaining Hugo Story demo sections and assets are omitted.
