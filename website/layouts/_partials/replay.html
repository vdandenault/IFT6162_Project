How we checked the numbers

We reproduced three complete runs for each year at coupled-study revision {{ substr $run.revision 0 7 }}. We matched the initial state, inflows and issued forecasts across controllers and checked all 48 executed quarter-months in each run. The baseline applies the simulator’s safety filter to the Plan 2014 release proposal.

Spring covers quarter-months 13–24. Flood exposure sums the positive difference between each level and its study threshold over those twelve periods. We checked annual exposure, cost, hydropower value and final Ottawa storage against the saved run summaries. Building counts are peak modeled estimates across the indicator sites in each region.

We paired forecast-based and Stackelberg MPC by year and quarter-month and compared executed releases before rounding. We also replayed each decision and inspected both scores for every eligible schedule. Each scoring rule reproduced its saved first release. Both controllers faced the same Ottawa operator and the same realized inflows when those releases were executed. Candidate scoring

The simulator reports hydropower value and recreational boating impacts in US dollars. We sum its quarter-month indicators for April through June in their original currency. Annual operating cost is a separate weighted score in objective units. Hydropower units · Boating units
