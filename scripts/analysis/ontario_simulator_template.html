How MPC controls this system

MPC plans four releases, applies the first through the Bv7 flow limits, then replans from the resulting state. Its current objective tracks rule-curve releases and forecast terminal storage. When the search finds no schedule meeting both caps, it prioritizes peak and then total exceedance.
